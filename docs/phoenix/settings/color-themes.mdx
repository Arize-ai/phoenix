---
title: "Color Themes"
description: "Change the color theme of the Phoenix UI"
---

Change the color theme of the Phoenix UI. Phoenix has four color themes:

- **Light**: White surfaces with dark text.
- **Dark**: Near-black surfaces with light text. This is the default theme.
- **Sunshine**: Golden yellow surfaces with dark text. It is a variant of the Light theme.
- **System**: Uses the Light or Dark theme to match the setting of your operating system. System never selects Sunshine.

Phoenix saves the theme in this browser only. Phoenix does not carry the theme to other browsers or devices.

## Change the theme in the account menu

1. In the side navigation, click **Account**.
2. Hover over an icon at the bottom of the menu to see the name of its theme.
3. Click the icon for the theme that you want.

## Change the theme in your profile

1. In the side navigation, click **Account**.
2. Click **Profile**.
3. Click the **Preferences** tab.
4. In **Theme**, select a theme.

In **Theme**, System appears as **Auto (light)** or **Auto (dark)**. The label shows the current setting of your operating system.
