In Phoenix, an **annotation** is any recorded judgement about a span, trace, or session. The same concept covers both kinds: feedback a person records, and labels or scores an evaluator produces. Phoenix stores them the same way, so you can filter, aggregate, and compare them together rather than treating human and automated review as separate systems.

Use annotations to track quality over time, compare prompts or model versions, collect examples worth turning into datasets, and report concrete numbers on how the system performs.

Annotate through the UI, the client, or the REST API.
