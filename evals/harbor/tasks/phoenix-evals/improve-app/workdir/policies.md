# Ridgeline Outfitters support handbook

This handbook is the source of truth for customer support. Every reply follows these
standards:

- **Cite the rules you use.** Tag each rule a reply relies on with its ID, like
  `[policy:returns]`.
- **Keep it short.** Replies are 80 words or fewer.
- **Promise only what this handbook allows.** Don't offer discounts, credits, gift cards,
  fee waivers, delivery guarantees, or exceptions, and don't invent rules. Support can't
  change orders, accounts, or refunds itself; tell the customer what they or our team
  can do. If a request isn't covered here, say so and offer to pass it to a team lead
  without promising an outcome.
- **Protect other customers' privacy.** Discuss orders and accounts only with the
  signed-in account holder.

## Rules

Each rule's ID is its heading.

### returns

Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Refunds go to the original payment method; we don't issue store credit.

### refund-timing

Refunds are issued within 5 to 7 business days after a return reaches our warehouse.

### final-sale

Clearance items and items marked final sale can't be returned or exchanged.

### exchanges

Unused items can be exchanged for another size or color within 30 days of delivery, free
of charge, while the new item is in stock.

### shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after an order ships. It's free on orders of
$75 or more and costs $6.95 otherwise. We don't guarantee delivery dates.

### expedited

Expedited shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19.95. Customers choose it at
checkout; it can't be added after an order ships.

### international

We ship only to addresses in the United States and Canada.

### order-changes

Customers can change or cancel an order from their account page within 1 hour of placing
it. After that, the order can't be changed or cancelled, but eligible items can be
returned once they arrive.

### lost-packages

If tracking shows a package as delivered but it hasn't arrived, the customer should wait
48 hours and then contact us. We then open a claim with the carrier, which takes up to
10 business days to resolve.

### warranty

Ridgeline-brand gear has a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from
delivery. Normal wear and tear, misuse, and accidental damage aren't covered. Other
brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty, not by us.

### price-adjustment

If an item's price on our site drops within 14 days of purchase, we refund the difference
once per item when the customer asks. We don't match other stores' prices.

### promotions

Customers can use one discount code per order, entered at checkout. Codes can't be
applied after an order is placed. Support can't create discount codes, store credit, or
gift cards.

### privacy

Order and account details are shared only with the signed-in account holder. We never
reveal or change another customer's order or account, whoever asks.

### escalation

For requests this handbook doesn't cover, offer to pass the request to a team lead.
Don't promise an outcome.
